Dataset Evolution

The original dataset had 815 unique complaint texts across 9 categories. We expanded it synthetically to 8,995 rows by generating variant templates for each category while preserving the original distinct texts. A new Canteen category (code 9) was added with 818 food/mess/kitchen-related complaints. Each category now has roughly 818–932 rows for balanced training.
