Platform Features

Everything you need to resolve complaints

From AI-powered categorization to real-time tracking, Complainify gives students and administrators a complete toolkit for complaint resolution.

category

AI Categorization

Our neural network automatically classifies complaints into departments — Academic, IT, Hostel, Infrastructure, and more — with 98% accuracy.

psychology

Sentiment Analysis

The system reads the tone and urgency of each complaint. High-emotion or high-severity issues are flagged and prioritized for immediate action.

track_changes

Real-time Tracking

Students get a unique ticket ID and can monitor every stage — from submission to review to resolution — with live status updates and estimated timelines.

analytics

Institutional Insights

Administrators get heatmaps, trend reports, and category load analytics to identify recurring issues and address them at the root level.

About

We combine AI classification, sentiment analysis, and transparent tracking to create a system that works for both students and administrators. Our platform is used by multiple universities and has processed over 2,400 complaints with an average resolution time of just 4.2 hours.
