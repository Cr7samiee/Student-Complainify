Why 1,800 → 815?

The raw dataset merged two sources — complaint_triage (800 rows) and query_test (1,000 rows). Many complaint texts appeared multiple times (e.g., "LMS is not allowing assignment upload" appeared 15 times). Keeping duplicates would bias the ML model toward repeated texts, so we kept only 1 copy per unique complaint. Each text has a category label (9 types) and priority label (High/Medium/Low) assigned. 265 synthetic complaints were generated to balance under-represented categories (Library: 1→100, Academics: 114→150, Admin: 100→150, IT: 100→150, Security: 100→130).
