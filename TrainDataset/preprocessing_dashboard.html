Step 1: Data Consolidation

The raw dataset contained 1,800 complaint records merged from two sources — complaint_triage (800 rows) and query_test (1,000 rows). Each row has a complaint text, a category label (9 types), and a priority label (High/Medium/Low). After removing text-level redundancy (keeping only unique complaint texts), the dataset was reduced to 815 unique records.
